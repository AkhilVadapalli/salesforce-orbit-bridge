# Salesforce AI Business Solutions

A portfolio repository focused on building business-first Salesforce applications that combine workflow automation, AI, integrations, and product thinking.

## Mission

This repo exists to show that Salesforce development can go far beyond standard Apex and LWC examples. The goal is to build solutions that solve real business pain points, improve operations, and remain relevant as AI and automation reshape enterprise software.

## Why this matters

The strongest long-term value in the Salesforce ecosystem is not just writing code. It is building systems that:

- improve response times
- reduce operational friction
- connect data across platforms
- create AI-assisted decision support
- work like real business products, not isolated demos

## Portfolio focus

- Salesforce platform architecture
- AI-enhanced operational workflows
- process automation and orchestration
- integration and API-driven architecture
- AppExchange-style solution design
- business value and ROI thinking

## Current portfolio direction

## Priority project

### AI Case Triage Assistant

A Salesforce-based business workflow to:

- understand incoming support cases faster
- summarize activity history
- identify urgency and likely support area
- recommend routing and next steps
- draft response suggestions for agents
- escalate severe or risky cases automatically

This project is a strong fit for a modern Salesforce portfolio because it combines business value, AI usefulness, and operational automation.
