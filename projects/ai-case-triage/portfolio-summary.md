# Portfolio Summary

## Project title

AI Case Triage Assistant

## Category

Salesforce + AI + workflow automation + support operations

## Problem solved

Support teams often struggle with case overload, inconsistent prioritization, and delayed routing decisions.

## Solution delivered

This project delivers a Salesforce workflow that summarizes case context, recommends priority, suggests routing, and supports agent response generation.

## What is included

- business problem framing
- architecture design
- requirement definition
- Apex service logic
- LWC triage panel
- test coverage
- deployment and testing plan
- AppExchange readiness notes

## Why this is valuable

It shows the ability to think beyond basic Salesforce custom development and build practical business solutions that are useful in real operations.

## How it fits the long-term strategy

This project represents the kind of work that remains relevant over time because it sits at the intersection of:

- Salesforce CRM
- AI-assisted operations
- scalable business workflows
- enterprise productivity improvement

## Final takeaway

This project is a strong example of how to build market-oriented Salesforce apps that solve real business problems and align with the future of AI-enabled enterprise software.
